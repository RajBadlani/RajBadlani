  ## 👋 Hey, I'm Raj

  I'm a final-year student and **full-stack developer** who got hooked on code the moment I realized I could turn an idea in my head into something real
  that people could actually use. It started with me pulling apps apart to figure out how they worked under the hood — and somewhere along the way, building
  them became the fun part.

  These days I work across the whole stack: the **APIs and databases** on the backend, the **interface** on the front, and a layer of **AI** wherever it
  makes a product genuinely smarter instead of just buzzword-y. I learn best by shipping — every project teaches me something no tutorial ever did.

  > 🌱 Currently going deeper into **System Design**, **distributed systems**, and building **production-ready full-stack apps**.
